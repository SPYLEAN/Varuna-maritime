## 2. Policy Directives & Prohibitions

1. **Attribution Preservation**: Original copyright notices, docstrings, and license blocks must remain present in all source files adapting or incorporating third-party logic.
2. **Canonical Discovery Separation**: VARUNA's `sentinel_catalog.py` remains the canonical discovery mechanism against the current Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (CDSE) STAC API. Third-party ingest routines are adapted strictly for product URL resolution, authentication token negotiation, and resumable streaming product downloads (`attached observation -> resolve product -> download_product`).
3. **Slicksmith Pseudo-Timestamps Notice**: The pseudo-timestamps generated by `slicksmith_ttom` routines (e.g. artificial year 2050 timestamps) are **ML indexing metadata ONLY** to prevent TorchGeo temporal collisions. They must **NEVER** be used as Sentinel acquisition timestamps, legal evidence timestamps, or VARUNA case provenance.
4. **OpenDrift Isolation**: `OpenDrift` is licensed under GPLv2 and remains an external integration. Its code is **NOT** vendored or copied into the VARUNA repository.
5. **KrishnanDhalavai Exclusion**: `KrishnanDhalavai/SAR-Oil-Spill-Detection-Segmentation-SegFormer-B0` contains no explicit open-source license and is **NOT** copied or vendored.
6. **NOAA PyGNOME**: Reserved as public-domain reference for future cross-model physical validation; not integrated at this phase.
7. **Pipeline Designation**: The open-source pipeline integrated herein is labeled **`VARUNA QUICKLOOK SAR ANALYSIS`**. Later ESA SNAP processing will be designated **`VARUNA ANALYSIS-GRADE SAR PROCESSING`**.
